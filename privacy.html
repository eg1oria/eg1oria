Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Summary

Clipper runs entirely on the user's own computer. It has no servers, does not collect analytics, and does not sell or share any data.

What data Clipper handles

  • Your videos. Videos and the clips made from them are stored and processed only on your computer.
  • TikTok account data. When you sign in with TikTok, Clipper receives your TikTok display name, avatar and an access token (scopes user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish). They are stored only on your computer, in a file readable only by your user account.
  • Captions and settings you enter for a post are sent to TikTok together with the clip you chose to upload.

How the data is used

TikTok data is used only to show which account is connected and to upload the clips you explicitly choose to that account. Clipper sends data only to TikTok, and only when you confirm an upload.

Sharing

Clipper does not share data with anyone other than TikTok, and only as described above.

Removing access and data

Disconnect your account in Clipper — this revokes the access token and deletes it from your computer. You can also remove Clipper's access in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. Deleting the Clipper folder removes all of its data.
