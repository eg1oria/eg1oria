Clipper

A personal desktop tool that turns my long videos into short vertical clips and uploads them to my own TikTok account.

What it does

  • Runs locally on my computer. Videos are processed on the device; nothing is uploaded to third-party servers.
  • Finds the most engaging moments in a long video, cuts them into 9:16 clips and adds subtitles.
  • With my permission, sends the clips I choose to my TikTok account — as drafts to the TikTok inbox, or as direct posts — using the official TikTok Content Posting API.

How the TikTok integration works

  1. I sign in with TikTok (Login Kit) and approve access.
  2. I pick a clip, review the caption and privacy settings, and confirm the upload.
  3. The clip is uploaded to my TikTok account and I can see its status in the app.
